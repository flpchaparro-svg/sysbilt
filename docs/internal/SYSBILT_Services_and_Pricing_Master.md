## How to use this document

**The boundary.** Everything in here is internal. Prices, time estimates, retainer logic, and the tool stack stay inside the team. They go into quotes, proposals, and tax invoices, never onto the website, a guide, or a blog post. Reader-facing copy can describe what a service does. It never carries a price or a revenue figure.

**Naming convention.** Every service and subtask has a plain functional name and a one-line outcome tag underneath. The name says what it is. The tag says what the client gets and sets the expectation.

**How to read a price.** Every range has a low end and a high end. Quote the low end when the inputs are clean, the build is templated, it sits on one platform, and the client is on our preferred stack. Quote the high end when the data is messy or legacy, the integration is custom, there's a compliance requirement, or the client is locked into a platform we have to adapt to. Time estimates assume the client has their inputs ready. Add time when you're chasing them for logins, content, or sign-off.

**Where we sit in the market (for holding the line in a quote).**
- Australian websites run roughly $4,000 to $15,000 for a professional build, custom and headless higher again. Our floor sits just below that, so we're competitive without looking cheap.
- HubSpot partner implementations run $4,000 to $9,000. Our fast, AI-accelerated CRM setups come in under that and deliver in days.
- Business automation projects run $5,000 to $20,000 mid-range. Our pillar automations sit inside that band.
- AI assistants on a rapid-deploy, low-code stack run $5,000 to $15,000 in the local market, with bespoke builds far higher. This is where we'd been underpriced. The new numbers reflect it.
- SEO and content retainers run $1,500 to $5,000 a month. Our content work matches.
- Traditional video runs $5,000 to $20,000 a shoot. AI video cuts that by most of the way, which is the whole pitch for our studio work.

## The four tiers (the pyramid)

The structure narrows as commitment rises. The wide base is fast, transactional work where cash lands quickly. The apex is a long, contract-based relationship.

| Tier | What it is | Commitment | Cash rhythm |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Digital Transformation** | Annual embedded partnership. We run the systems side of the business. | 12 months, one client at a time | Annual / monthly retainer |
| **2. Phase Deployment** | A bundle that delivers a whole phase: Get Clients, Scale Faster, or See Clearly. | Multi-week project | Staged on milestones |
| **3. Pillar Service** | The main build inside one pillar. | One to a few weeks | Deposit + completion |
| **4. Subtask** | A single à la carte job. One to two weeks or less. | Short | Paid up front, cash straight away |

**The ceiling rule for subtasks.** A subtask is anything one person can scope and deliver in one to two weeks, with no open-ended commitment. The moment a job needs more than two weeks, ongoing engineering, or a long relationship, it stops being a subtask and becomes a pillar service. That rule keeps the base of the pyramid clean.

## The retainer ladder

Every build can carry a retainer. It's the natural next step after a project, not an add-on. Three rungs, attach the right one to the work.

| Rung | What it covers | Typical monthly (AUD) | Attach to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Care** | Hosting, SSL, security, monitoring, broken-tracking fixes, light updates | $150 – $500 | Any website, CRM, chat or phone agent, dashboard |
| **Ops** | Active optimisation: pipeline, email, content, conversion experiments | $500 – $1,500 | Pipelines, email engines, content systems, conversion audits |
| **Fractional** | Embedded ownership: RevOps, Digital Ops, or CDO running the system | $1,500 – $3,000+ | Operations audits, private AI, full data architecture |

**How to position it.** Quote the build and the retainer together. The build gets the system live. The retainer keeps it working as tools change, models drift, and the business grows. Two anchors from the market: managed IT runs $120 to $280 per user per month, and an in-house automation engineer costs north of $130,000 a year, so a $1,500 to $3,000 retainer that runs the whole system is easy to justify. For Tier 1 Digital Transformation the retainer is folded into the annual fee, so don't quote it separately there.

## Quoting checklist

1. Which tier is this: a subtask, a pillar service, a phase bundle, or the full transformation.
2. Are the inputs clean and is the client on our stack (low end of the range) or messy, legacy, or on a platform we adapt to (high end).
3. Time estimate, plus a buffer if we'll be chasing them for logins, content, or sign-off.
4. Which retainer rung attaches, quoted alongside the build.
5. Confirm no price or revenue figure leaks into anything reader-facing. Prices live in the quote, the proposal, and the tax invoice only.
