# SYSBILT Cold Emails, V2

Version 2.0, 29 July 2026. Rebuilt on the mechanics from Felipe's own draft.

Source of truth for outbound Gmail drafts (Audit gift, Website, Speed Fix, and Template C product lanes).

## Rules

1. **"I" for what you observed, "we" for what the firm does.** "I opened your site" then "we build and host".
2. **No scores in the email.** Never "speed 35". Say what it means. Numbers live in the audit or test.
3. **Always explain why they never noticed** (cache, own-name search, silent missed call, etc.).
4. **Link inside a sentence, not as a button.**
5. **Signature is two lines.** Name, firm. No tagline, no Sydney descriptor.
6. **Short sentences.**
7. **The ask is small and isn't buying.** Reply is the conversion.
8. **Never hyperlink their own domain.**

**Greeting.** Verified first name only when Owner Name has two or more words. Single-token fields are often surnames. Otherwise `Hi,`. Never invent. Never "Hi there".

## Template A: The Audit

Subject: `I had a look at {Business}`

Gift only. One report link. No product links. Soft no-thanks footer.

## Template B: The site needs replacing

Subject: `{Business} on a phone`

Cache blind spot. Hosted Website Plan link inside a sentence. Offer a short note on reply.

## Template C: One fixable problem

Subject: problem stated flatly (e.g. `{Business} loads slowly on phones`).

Observation → why they never noticed → what we do + product page → small ask.
