## What we are

A system architecture and automation firm. Functionally close to an agency in scope, but we do not sell software, we do not white-label other vendors, and we are not a software-as-a-service product. We build, hand over, and provide optional ongoing support.

We do not "do content" or "do social media" as a service. What we do is help businesses show who they actually are, fast, by building the systems that produce and run it.

**Marketing stance.** SYSBILT does not run paid ads for itself. Growth comes from search, content, and referrals, and Built to Multiply states this plainly. Client-side ad reporting and attribution (dashboards, tracking) are fine to discuss; never imply SYSBILT grows through ads.

**Stop doing everyone's job.** It can live anywhere it fits and it earns repetition.

## The differentiator

Connection-sight. We see the dots a business will need to connect later. You can buy one piece now or the whole system over time. Sooner or later the connections will need to happen, and connecting them is what we do.

## Audience

Growing Australian companies, premium SMB operators, mostly between $1M and $20M revenue. The band is internal only and never appears in reader-facing copy. It is for filtering and targeting, not promotion. Example verticals: clinics and healthcare practices, professional services firms, high-end builders, prestige brands, and established retail and wholesale operators.

The audience is non-technical: they don't know AI and don't care about mechanics. They care about time back, money saved, stress reduced, and health protected.

**Framing rules, non-negotiable:**

- The reader-facing frame is "growing businesses" and "growing companies". This is live on the site's meta as of July 2026 and carries through all copy and SEO work.
- Never describe the audience as "small businesses", in copy or in keyword targets. The March 2026 keyword set targeting "small business" phrases is retired.
- Never frame clients as tradies, plumbers, or blue-collar operators, and never use blue-collar idioms ("on the tools"). High-end builders are in the audience; the framing is premium operator, not trade.

## Tools

We are not married to any vendor. In-house we use n8n, and we can use Make.com or Zapier depending on the client and what they're invested in. What we run in-house is irrelevant to our content.

Content names the tools the reader uses and searches for, objectively and educationally. If the keyword is Make.com, the post is about Make.com. If readers use GPT or Gemini, we write about those. We mention our own tools only occasionally, in a specific post where it's genuinely the point, never as a default. Relational content (email, SMS) names outcomes, not tools.

## The Systems Audit

The standing CTA on cold surfaces (social, website) is a free intro conversation, branded the "Systems Audit". The paid audit (the Technical Audit and Strategy at the top of the pricing ladder) is a deeper engagement that comes after qualification, never the cold CTA. The entry is always free.
