# SYSBILT copy

Read these before writing reader-facing words:

1. `docs/internal/BRAND_FACTS.md` (wins on contradictions)
2. `docs/internal/CHANNEL_PLAYBOOK.md` (channel shape)
3. Voice Step 5 in `docs/internal/TOOLKIT_POST_AGENT_INSTRUCTION.md`

Note: `BRAND_VOICE_CORE.md` is referenced in docs but may be missing from the repo. Until it is restored, enforce the checklist below plus BRAND_FACTS.

## Hard rules (break none)

| Rule | Do this instead |
|------|-----------------|
| No em dashes (`—`) | Comma, colon, or new sentence |
| No exclamation marks | Full stop or rewrite |
| No periods on headlines | Title case / sentence without final `.` |
| No emojis | Plain text |
| Australian English | analyse, organise, optimise, behaviour, colour, centre |
| "We" / "you" | Never company "I" (except Felipe personal LinkedIn) |
| Contractions on | we're, you'll, it's |
| No "small business" | growing business / companies / Australian businesses |
| No pillar numbers | Service names only |
| No public prices | Except locked `/go/` funnel exceptions |

## Banned hype words

leverage, streamline, seamless, robust, harness, unlock, transform, navigate (figurative), empower, comprehensive, synergy, holistic, cutting-edge, game-changing, revolutionise, supercharge, world-class, mission-critical, deep dive, tap into, unleash, foster, facilitate, ecosystem, bandwidth, value-add, ideate, learnings, actionable insights, drive growth, take to the next level, delve, elevate, moves the needle, best-in-class, turnkey, frictionless, at scale, next-level.

## Tone

- Warm, confident, premium, plain. Pub test: a 16-year-old or a friend at the pub follows it.
- Direct, a little blunt, not startup hype.
- Outcome for the owner (time, money, stress), not feature dumps.
- If you must use a term like CMS, add one plain-English gloss in the same breath.

## Pre-ship checklist

```
- [ ] No — or -- used as em dash
- [ ] No !
- [ ] No US -ize/-yze or color/behavior
- [ ] No banned hype words
- [ ] We/you voice, contractions on
- [ ] No small business / pillar numbers / unapproved prices
- [ ] Jargon explained where used
```

## Examples

Bad: `We ran your site — it scored 28 — and we're ready!`  
Good: `We ran your site. It scored 28 on mobile.`

Bad: `Unlock seamless, cutting-edge performance.`  
Good: `Your pages load fast enough that people stay and book.`
