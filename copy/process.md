## The 4 phases

/ THE 4 PHASES  

How every project works  

01 / DIAGNOSE  
Find the leaks  
Where does your data get typed twice? Where do your leads go cold? Where does profit disappear? We find it.  

02 / DESIGN  
Pick the right tools  
No platform lock-in. We pick the software that actually fits your business and map the logic before writing a single line of code.  

03 / BUILD  
Build in sprints  
No six-month projects that drain your budget. We work in short sprints. You see progress in weeks, not quarters.  

04 / HANDOVER  
Make it stick  
Software fails when people don't use it. We handle the training, support, and check-ins until your team actually adopts it.
