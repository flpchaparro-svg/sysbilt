## FAQ

Questions about content  

Common questions about what we create, how it works, and where it gets published  

Do I have to be on camera for this to work  
No. We work with whatever you're comfortable with. Voice memos, written notes, a phone call where we ask you questions. We extract your expertise in the format that suits you. If you hate video, we don't do video.  

Can you create content that sounds like me  
Yes, that's the whole point. We calibrate the tone until it matches how you actually talk. The content sounds like you wrote it, not like a marketing agency churned it out.  

How much content can this system produce  
One 30-minute conversation can become a long-form blog post, five social media updates, an email newsletter, and a script. One input, many outputs.  

Does this replace my marketing team  
It replaces the production work of creating content. It doesn't replace high-level strategy or community management. Think of it as a production line that handles the heavy lifting so your team can focus on the thinking.  

Which platforms can this post to  
Most of them. LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, YouTube, and your blog. We set up auto-posting so you approve once and it goes everywhere.
