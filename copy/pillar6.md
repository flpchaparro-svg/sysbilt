## The gap

/ WHAT MOST PEOPLE MISS  

The problem was never the software  

Most businesses fail at the last step. They buy the tools but never get the team across the line.  

THE GAP  
The expensive shelfware  
You invested in a new CRM, a new process, or a new system and sent an email saying "we're switching to this now." A month later half the team is still using their personal spreadsheet. They don't trust the new tool, or they think it's too hard, or nobody showed them how it fits into their actual day. So they quietly go back to the old way and your investment sits there doing nothing.  

THE GAP  
The training that doesn't stick  
You've explained the same process a dozen times. You've run the induction and sent the PDF and they still get it wrong. It's not because your team is lazy, it's because a one-hour session or a 30-page manual is not how people actually learn. They forget most of it by the next morning and you end up re-explaining the same things every week.

## Solution

/ HOW WE BUILD IT  

Training your team will actually use  

We don't hand you a document and wish you luck. We build training in the formats people actually engage with and we make sure it sticks.  

SCALE FASTER  
01  
Short videos, not long meetings  
A 3-minute screen recording beats a 3-hour workshop. We build bite-sized training your team can watch when they're stuck, rewatch when they forget, and access from their phone. You explain each process once and it works forever.  

SCALE FASTER  
02  
The reason before the rollout  
If your team doesn't understand why something is changing they will resist it. We help you communicate the reason behind the change before you launch the tool. When people understand how it makes their day easier they stop fighting it.  

SCALE FASTER  
03  
An AI mentor that never gets tired of answering  
When someone is stuck they don't want to look foolish asking you. We set up an internal AI assistant trained on your processes that walks them through the task using your exact rules. They get the answer instantly and you don't get interrupted.  

SCALE FASTER  
04  
New hires onboard themselves  
Every time you hire someone you lose a day training them. With a library of short videos and a knowledge base they can search, new staff get up to speed in weeks instead of months. You train once and every future hire benefits.

## FAQ

Questions about training  

Common questions about how we get your team to actually use new tools  

My team hates new software, how do you get them to use it  
Resistance usually comes from fear that it means more work. We make adoption easy with short videos and clear guides, and we show them the direct benefit to their day. We don't disappear after training, we stay until they're actually using it.  

Do you train everyone or just the manager  
Everyone who uses the system gets trained. Managers get the strategy view and staff get the day-to-day workflow view. We also create materials for future hires so you don't have to re-train every time someone new joins.  

Is the training live, videos, or documents  
A mix of all three. Short rewatchable videos for the core processes, a live walkthrough for questions and rollout, and written documentation for reference. We don't do 3-hour webinars. Your team watches a 3-minute video when they're stuck.  

What happens when I hire someone new after the project  
They use the same training library. Videos, docs, and the AI knowledge base. You onboard new staff without paying us again. The training is yours to keep.  

How long until my team is comfortable with new systems  
Simple tools take a week or two. More complex systems take about a month. We start small, add features over time, and don't overwhelm them on day one.
